Blend Book is planned as an offline notebook for home cooks who make their own spice blends. Save ingredient measures and tasting revisions, scale a chosen revision into a new batch, and make a jar label that records what went into it. Export a backup to keep your recipes in your own hands. Designed for phone, tablet, and Windows desktop. Development is in progress; these features are planned and not yet available. No account or cloud sync is planned. Always check ingredients yourself for allergens.
