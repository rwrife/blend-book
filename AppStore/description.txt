Blend Book is an offline notebook for home cooks who make their own spice blends. Save measured ingredients and dated tasting revisions without overwriting earlier recipes. Scale a selected revision into a batch worksheet, then export a printable PDF jar card with its ingredients, revision, and batch date. Export a versioned JSON backup; restore with validation and an undo snapshot. Works on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, and Windows desktop. Blends stay on your device: no account, cloud sync, tracking, or required network access. Always check ingredients yourself for allergens.
